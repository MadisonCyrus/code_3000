
**Accountability**

As a student I am always learning about AI and its applications. As I have started implementing what I've learned, I realize I have a responsibility for the design, deployment, impacts, and oversight of the AI technologies I develop. I believe accountability is essential because AI technology can have significant consequences for individuals and society, and developers must take responsibility for those outcomes.


I will prioritize safe outcomes before I deploy AI technology. I will consider potential risks, identifying potential consequences, and ensure that the systems I develop are designed to benefit users without causing harm.

I will demonstrate accountability across the AI lifecycle. That means during development I will make thoughtful decisions on training data, model design and safety testing. During deployment I will create appropriate access controls and monitor performance and results. During operation I will maintain ongoing oversight and incident response.
